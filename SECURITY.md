# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please tell us before publishing it.

**[security@vkxtech.com.br](mailto:security@vkxtech.com.br)**

If you use VKX Wallet, the in-app route is better: **Settings → Support Center →
Create ticket**, category **Security**. It is authenticated, keeps the thread,
and already tells us the app version and device.

Include the steps to reproduce and the impact you see. A proof of concept helps.

## What to expect

| Timeline | Commitment |
| --- | --- |
| 2 business days | Confirmation that we received and read it |
| 7 days | Our severity assessment and a plan |
| 90 days | Fix published, or an explanation of why not yet |

Reports do not go through automated triage. They reach a person.

## What we ask

- Do not exploit further than necessary to demonstrate the flaw.
- Do not access, alter, or exfiltrate other people's data. Use your own accounts.
- Do not run denial-of-service or load testing.
- Talk to us first. Publishing before a fix exists exposes people using the
  wallet, including those with no way to protect themselves. Ask for whatever
  deadline you need.

## What you get

We do not run a monetary bounty programme, and we would rather say so than
advertise amounts we would not honour.

What we do offer: a fast response, a published fix, and credit for the finding —
your name, a pseudonym, or **your wallet address**, which makes the credit
verifiable, since you can prove authorship at any time by signing a message with
the same wallet. Anonymous is fine too.

We will not pursue legal action against good-faith research that follows the
rules above.

## Scope

This repository covers encryption at rest and PIN key derivation. Issues in the
wallet application, the website, or VKX smart contracts are equally welcome
through the same channels.

The full policy, including our threat model and the limits of what this
protection covers, is at
[wallet.vkxtech.com.br/wallet-security](https://wallet.vkxtech.com.br/wallet-security).
